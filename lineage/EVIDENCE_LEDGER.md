The program does not average evidence across maturity levels. Downstream authority depends on upstream gates, valid causal composition, and scope-limited interpretation of trajectory and repertoire evidence.

## Research Returnability authority detail

The `CCA-RETURN` decision gains authority only for disciplined reopening of current research authority.

```text
AUTHORITY GAINED
  current conceptual, methodological, and scientific authority states
  are reopenable when new discriminating evidence reaches them

AUTHORITY NOT GAINED
  retrospective rewriting of closed empirical results or executed contracts
  reopening from mere redescription
  automatic escalation after successful localization
  empirical authority for G1, PMC, repeated correction, JT, C_improve,
  viability, AGI, or ASI
  implementation or execution authorization

PRESERVED
  historical result identity
  prospective contract discipline
  anti-rescue rule
  shallowest-sufficient localization
  stopping rule
  execution-authorization requirements

REOPENING CONDITION
  new discriminating evidence reaches the Research Returnability Rule itself

STOPPING CONDITION
  localized repair survives discrimination and retest with no residual
```

## Research maturity ladder

```text
LEVEL 0  measurement/scientific-object validity  G1 ROLE PROVISIONALLY FIXED; CONTRACT UNFROZEN
LEVEL 1  evidence-controlled adaptive decision   SCIENTIFIC OBJECT DEFINED; EMPIRICALLY UNTESTED
LEVEL 2  isolated modification                    ARCHITECTURE ONLY
LEVEL 3  evidence → justified modification        NOT AUTHORIZED; CAUSAL COMPOSITION REQUIRED
LEVEL 4A post-modification correctability         ROLE PROVISIONALLY FIXED; METRIC/CONTRACT UNFROZEN
LEVEL 4B repeated correction                      CONCEPTUALLY DISTINCT; EMPIRICALLY UNTESTED
LEVEL 5  justified transformability               ROLE PROVISIONALLY FIXED; WARRANT/DISTINCTNESS UNDER REVIEW
LEVEL 6  adaptive viability / capability          NOT AUTHORIZED
LEVEL 7  extreme adaptive systems                 NOT AUTHORIZED
```

The ladder is causal/evidential, not additive. Its ordering is not automatically a universal logical definition of each downstream object.

## Current conceptual frontier

> **Can the warrant, distinctness, repertoire, preservation, target-family, and counterfactual-availability semantics of Justified Transformability be specified without retrospective approval, trivial-difference inflation, or benchmark-defined reachability?**

This is a scientific-object frontier, not authorization to execute a transformability experiment.

## Evidence-to-authority rule

Evidence updates only the authority dimensions it can identify. A realized trajectory does not authorize a broader repertoire claim unless that repertoire was prospectively constituted and measured. A large state space does not establish a warranted transformation repertoire.
