# Decision: Justified Transformability Role

## Status

**PROVISIONAL CONCEPTUAL ROLE — WARRANT / DISTINCTNESS SEMANTICS UNFROZEN — NO METRIC — NO EMPIRICAL RESULT — NO EXECUTION AUTHORIZED**

## Decision

Correction-Capable Adaptation provisionally adopts **Justified Transformability (JT)** as a distinct repertoire-level conceptual object downstream of repeated correction.

Its current scientific role is:

> **What prospectively relevant warranted transformations remain reachable from a system state, and can materially different warranted destinations be reached while preserving or reconstituting the causal conditions required for future warranted correction?**

This role is intentionally broader than a realized correction trajectory and narrower than adaptive viability.

## Three-way distinction

CCA now preserves three different objects:

\[
\boxed{\mathrm{PMC}=\text{availability of future warranted correction}}
\]

\[
\boxed{\mathrm{RepeatedCorrection}=\text{realized temporal exercise}}
\]

\[
\boxed{\mathrm{JT}=\text{warranted transformation repertoire}}
\]

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{\mathrm{PMC}\not\Rightarrow\mathrm{RepeatedCorrection}}
\]

\[
\boxed{\mathrm{RepeatedCorrection}\not\Rightarrow\mathrm{JT}}
\]

and a prospectively established repertoire claim need not imply that one physical instance has already traversed a repeated correction history:

\[
\boxed{\mathrm{JT}\not\Rightarrow\mathrm{ObservedRepeatedCorrectionHistory}}.
\]

The last relation prevents CCA's research evidence order from becoming a universal definition of the object.

## Trajectory evidence is not repertoire evidence

Repeated correction establishes realized temporal recurrence under the correction opportunities actually exercised.

It does not by itself establish:

- breadth of warranted future transformations;
- ability to reach materially different warranted destinations;
- capacity beyond a scripted or fixed finite correction menu;
- preservation or reconstitution of future correction pathways across those different transformations;
- resistance to cumulative narrowing or irreversible path-dependent traps.

Compactly:

\[
\boxed{\text{trajectory evidence}\neq\text{repertoire evidence}.}
\]

## Evidence-order versus logical-object order

CCA may continue to place repeated correction before JT in its research maturity ladder because repeated correction tests survival of correction through actual consequential temporal change.

That is an **epistemic / evidence-policy ordering**.

It is not a claim that repeated temporal history is logically constitutive of every possible JT object.

Thus:

\[
\boxed{\text{logical object dependency}\neq\text{program evidence-ordering dependency}.}
\]

## What is explicitly unfrozen

This decision does **not** freeze:

- the semantics of `warranted` for a transformation;
- the semantics of `materially different` or `appropriately different`;
- any target or transformation family;
- any reachable-state or reachable-transformation formalism;
- novelty or distance thresholds;
- breadth, diversity, coverage, or repertoire metrics;
- preservation versus reconstitution semantics;
- a system/apparatus boundary for transformation availability;
- counterfactual availability criteria;
- an estimand, estimator, threshold, or empirical protocol;
- `C_improve`;
- adaptive viability;
- implementation or execution.

The term `repertoire` is therefore role-level language, not a frozen mathematical representation.

## Warrant must remain prospective

JT must not degenerate into retrospective approval of whatever transformation happened to occur.

Any future JT contract will need a prospectively constituted relation between evidence / warrant and admissible transformation destinations before outcomes are observed.

Likewise, state diversity alone cannot establish JT.

\[
\boxed{\text{many reachable states}\not\Rightarrow\text{many warranted transformations}.}
\]

These boundaries motivate the next adversarial attack; they are not yet operational definitions.

## JT is not C_improve

JT asks what warranted transformation possibilities remain available and whether materially different warranted changes can preserve or reconstitute future correction pathways.

It does not claim that this repertoire improved because of prior feedback.

Therefore JT does not canonize `C_improve` and does not establish:

\[
\Delta \mathrm{JT}>0
\]

or:

\[
\Delta V_{\mathrm{future}}>0.
\]

Those are downstream claims.

## Authority gained

CCA may now use JT as a distinct conceptual object for **warranted transformation repertoire**, separate from PMC and repeated correction.

It may also explicitly distinguish trajectory evidence from repertoire evidence.

## Authority not gained

This decision does not establish:

- a JT metric;
- a positive JT result;
- a formal transformation space;
- a target-family definition;
- warrantedness semantics;
- distinctness semantics;
- preservation or reconstitution criteria;
- correction-capacity improvement;
- `C_improve`;
- adaptive viability;
- any implementation or execution authorization.

## Next conceptual frontier

The next attack is:

> **Can the warrant, distinctness, repertoire, preservation, target-family, and counterfactual-availability semantics of Justified Transformability be specified without retrospective approval, trivial-difference inflation, or benchmark-defined reachability?**

No empirical implementation follows from adopting the role.
