# Decision: PMC versus Repeated Correction

## Status

**CANONICAL CONCEPTUAL DISTINCTION — NO METRIC — NO EMPIRICAL RESULT — NO EXECUTION AUTHORIZED**

## Decision

Correction-Capable Adaptation canonically preserves the distinction:

\[
\boxed{
\mathrm{PMC}\neq\mathrm{Repeated\ Correction}
}
\]

with the following scoped roles:

- **Post-Modification Correctability (PMC):** after consequential change, the conditions required for future admissible evidence to acquire warranted causal authority over consequential correction remain available within a prospectively declared correction scope.
- **Repeated Correction:** those conditions are subsequently exercised in an additional valid correction episode established under its own prospective causal contract.

Compactly:

\[
\boxed{
\text{capacity survived}\neq\text{capacity exercised}.
}
\]

## Asymmetric relation

PMC is a latent/dispositional availability property. Repeated correction is a realized trajectory event.

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\mathrm{PMC}\not\Rightarrow\mathrm{ObservedRepeatedCorrection}.
}
\]

A system can remain correctable even when no future correction opportunity arrives inside the observation horizon.

Conversely, a valid repeated-correction episode provides only local authority:

\[
\boxed{
\mathrm{ValidRepeatedCorrection}(e)
\Rightarrow
\mathrm{LocalCorrectionAvailability}(e)
}
\]

for the realized opportunity \(e\), under the frozen system/apparatus boundary and path-valid causal process.

It does not by itself establish broad PMC over untested future opportunities:

\[
\boxed{
\mathrm{LocalCorrectionAvailability}(e)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathrm{PMC}(\Omega_{\mathrm{broader}}).
}
\]

## What repeated correction does not establish

A repeated-correction episode does not by itself establish:

- broad future correction capacity;
- novelty beyond a scripted or memorized trajectory;
- preservation across materially distinct correction opportunities;
- system-internal correctability when an external apparatus supplies the operative correction pathway;
- correction-capacity improvement;
- justified transformability.

Raw correction count is therefore not a correction-capacity metric:

\[
\boxed{
N_{\mathrm{corrections}}\neq C_{\mathrm{corr}}.
}
\]

## What PMC does not establish

PMC does not establish that another correction will actually occur. Realization additionally requires an opportunity plus valid activation/execution of the relevant correction path.

Schematic only:

\[
\boxed{
\mathrm{PMC}(S_{t+1};\Omega)
+\mathrm{Opportunity}
+\mathrm{ValidActivation/Execution}
\leadsto
\mathrm{RepeatedCorrection}.
}
\]

This is not a frozen causal model, metric, or estimand.

## Scientific dependency

CCA may now use the conceptual dependency:

\[
\boxed{
\text{one valid correction}
\rightarrow
\mathrm{PMC}
\rightarrow
\text{repeated correction}
}
\]

as a claim hierarchy, while recognizing that neither arrow is empirically established merely by adopting the concepts.

The first arrow asks whether correction capacity survives consequential change. The second concerns whether surviving capacity is actually exercised under a valid future correction opportunity.

## Authority gained

CCA may distinguish preservation of future warranted-correction capacity from realized repeated correction.

A valid repeated correction may support an opportunity-local availability claim, but scientific authority may not be generalized beyond the correction scope actually identified.

## Authority not gained

This decision does not establish or freeze:

- \(\Omega\) or any PMC environment;
- a system/apparatus boundary;
- a future horizon;
- a PMC metric or scalar;
- a repeated-correction metric;
- a required episode count;
- novelty or diversity criteria;
- a transformability metric;
- `C_improve`;
- an empirical protocol;
- any positive PMC or repeated-correction result;
- implementation or execution authorization.
