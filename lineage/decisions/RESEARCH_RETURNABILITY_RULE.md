# Decision: Research Returnability Rule

## Status

**CANONICAL METHODOLOGICAL RULE — REOPENABLE — NO EMPIRICAL RESULT — NO EXECUTION AUTHORIZED**

## Previous condition

Canonical and frozen language could be interpreted as permanent epistemic closure.

## Problem

Permanent epistemic immunity is incompatible with applying CCA's correction discipline to the research program itself.

A current authority state must be stable enough to govern present work without becoming immune to later discriminating evidence. Reopening must also preserve the identity of closed results and executed contracts rather than retroactively repairing them.

## Current rule

CCA adopts the Research Returnability Rule:

> **Open the smallest boundary implicated by new discriminating evidence; revise what the evidence reaches; preserve everything not implicated and its provenance; and retain a warranted path for future reopening.**

This preserves:

\[
\boxed{\text{local closure}+\text{global reopenability}}
\]

and:

\[
\boxed{\text{historical immutability}\neq\text{epistemic irreversibility}}
\]

## Authority gained

Current conceptual, methodological, and scientific authority states are reopenable when new discriminating evidence reaches them.

Reopening must proceed through the shallowest sufficient localization and an explicit provenance-preserving successor or amendment when object identity changes.

## Authority not gained

This rule does not grant:

- permission to rewrite closed empirical results;
- permission to alter executed contracts retrospectively;
- automatic reopening from deeper redescription;
- automatic escalation after successful localization;
- empirical evidence for G1, PMC, repeated correction, JT, `C_improve`, viability, AGI, or ASI;
- implementation or execution authorization.

## Preserved structure

The rule preserves:

- historical result identity;
- prospective contract discipline;
- the anti-rescue rule;
- shallowest-sufficient localization;
- the stopping rule;
- execution-authorization requirements.

\[
\boxed{\text{redescription}\neq\text{re-localization}}
\]

A successor or amendment may revise current authority. It must not silently rewrite the ancestor.

## Reopening condition

The Research Returnability Rule is itself reopenable when new discriminating evidence reaches it.

Any successor must identify the implicated boundary, preserve this record as provenance, and state which authority is withdrawn, retained, or transferred.

## Stopping condition

If the localized repair survives discrimination and retest and no discriminating residual remains, do not escalate further.

\[
\boxed{r=0\Rightarrow\text{stop escalation}}
\]
