## Lineage rule

A negative result may motivate diagnosis. Diagnosis may motivate a **new prospective scientific object**.

It may not rewrite the ancestor.

```text
ASI-0
  ↓
negative primary
  ↓
mechanism diagnosis
  ↓
new descendant object
```

The descendant must not be described as “ASI-0 fixed” or treated as a continuation that can change the original result.

## Descendant boundary

The provisionally fixed causal architecture motivated by the diagnosis is:

\[
E\rightarrow C_{\mathrm{selected}}\rightarrow M_{\mathrm{effective}}\rightarrow(Y_T,Y_P).
\]

The modification mechanism must be identified independently through:

\[
\boxed{do(M=m)\rightarrow(Y_T,Y_P)}.
\]

This design principle prevents efficacy from being inferred only from the subset of modifications a selector happened to choose.
