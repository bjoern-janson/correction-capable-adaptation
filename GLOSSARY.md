# Glossary

## Correction-capable adaptation

Warranted adaptive change that is effective, appropriately bounded, and preserves the conditions required for justified future correction.

## Evidence-conditioned selection

Candidate selection whose direction is warranted by independently interpretable evidence, rather than merely changing when the evidence changes.

## Protected behavior

Behavior outside the intended target of a modification that the scientific contract requires to remain within a prospectively declared tolerance.

## Target efficacy

The causal effect of a directly instantiated modification on its intended target outcome.

## Protected interference

The causal effect of a directly instantiated modification on protected behavior.

## Broad evidence use

A scientific object under which any independently warranted information legitimately contained in evidence may support selection.

## Relational evidence use

A stronger scientific object under which selection depends on the demonstrated input-output relation beyond a prospectively licensed class of non-relational information channels.

## Justified transformability

A provisional theoretical notion: the capacity to remain open to warranted future transformations after adapting. It is not yet an established metric.

## CARS

The research and reasoning protocol for localizing failures, discriminating explanations, revising minimally, retesting, preserving provenance, and stopping when authority is exhausted.

## MAGIKARP

The provisional theory layer for asking what makes one adaptive transformation more justified than another. It currently has no independent empirical authority.

## ASI-0

A historical evidence-assignment experiment. The name is inherited from the source research lineage; the experiment did **not** test ASI. It is closed with \(C=0\), \(A=0\), and `STOP`.

## ECIM

A provisional working label for the descendant evidence-controlled isolated-modification architecture. The name is not frozen; the causal contract matters more than the label.

## Frontier

The first unresolved causal prerequisite in the dependency graph. Program progress is not computed by averaging gate states.
