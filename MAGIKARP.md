# MAGIKARP

## Status

**Provisional theory layer — not an empirically validated construct, mechanism, or metric.**

MAGIKARP is a working theory surface for asking a higher-order question:

> **What could make one adaptive transformation more justified than another while preserving the possibility of future justified correction?**

CARS specifies how the research program responds to evidence. MAGIKARP organizes hypotheses about **transition quality**. It does not inherit empirical authority merely because those hypotheses are coherent.

## Current role

MAGIKARP may propose distinctions such as:

```text
evidence-responsive change
vs. arbitrary change

local efficacy
vs. justified efficacy

preservation
vs. mere inertia

current success
vs. retained future corrigibility

capability growth
vs. capability growth that destroys correction channels

state change
vs. reconstitution of future correction conditions
```

These remain theoretical distinctions until the corresponding scientific objects and measurement structures earn authority independently.

## Authority boundary

The project has previously failed to establish an empirically irreducible correction-specific primitive within an audited phenomenon space.

MAGIKARP therefore must not be introduced as though a distinct correction-quality ontology has already been demonstrated.

Keep explicit:

```text
useful theory vocabulary
≠ validated construct

candidate transition property
≠ metric

conceptual coherence
≠ empirical independence

unifying interpretation
≠ preferred failure locus
```

Any proposed MAGIKARP quantity or mechanism must pass the same Level-0 scientific-object and measurement discipline as every other object in CCA.

## Relationship to Justified Transformability

CCA now provisionally adopts **Justified Transformability (JT)** as a repertoire-level conceptual role:

> **What prospectively relevant warranted transformations remain reachable, and can materially different warranted destinations be reached while preserving or reconstituting future correction conditions?**

MAGIKARP may generate hypotheses relevant to JT, but it does not define JT by itself.

The following remain unfrozen for JT:

```text
warrant semantics
material / appropriate distinctness
transformation / target family
reachability formalism
preservation versus reconstitution
counterfactual availability
repertoire representation
metric / estimand / estimator / threshold
empirical protocol
```

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{\text{MAGIKARP coherence}\not\Rightarrow\text{JT validity}}
\]

and:

\[
\boxed{\text{many reachable states}\not\Rightarrow\text{many warranted transformations}}
\]

Decision record: [`lineage/decisions/JUSTIFIED_TRANSFORMABILITY_ROLE.md`](lineage/decisions/JUSTIFIED_TRANSFORMABILITY_ROLE.md).

## Returnability and theory

MAGIKARP is deliberately reopenable.

A useful theory can be superseded without rewriting the historical record that it once guided the program.

The repository-level discipline is documented in:

- [`KINTSUGI.md`](KINTSUGI.md)
- [`methodology/RESEARCH_RETURNABILITY.md`](methodology/RESEARCH_RETURNABILITY.md)

A deeper theoretical description does not reopen a successful local scientific diagnosis merely because it is more general:

\[
\boxed{\text{redescription}\neq\text{re-localization}}
\]

## Promotion burden

Before any MAGIKARP proposal becomes a measured scientific object, the program would need at least:

```text
scientific proposition
measurement structure
licensed transformation class
identified estimand
independent falsification
scope and causal boundary
incremental validity beyond ordinary state / dynamics / information / control descriptions
```

Until then, MAGIKARP remains a theory layer that generates attacks and candidate distinctions without borrowing empirical authority from the objects it is trying to explain.

\[
\boxed{\textbf{Maximum ambition; minimum unearned authority.}}
\]
