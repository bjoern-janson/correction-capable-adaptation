# MAGIKARP

## Status

**Provisional theory layer — not an empirically validated construct or metric.**

MAGIKARP is the working theoretical layer for asking a higher-order question:

> **What makes one adaptive transformation more justified than another?**

CARS specifies a correction process. MAGIKARP is intended to organize hypotheses about **transition quality**: when a change should count as warranted, appropriately bounded, and still compatible with future justified correction.

## Current role

MAGIKARP may propose distinctions such as:

```text
evidence-responsive change
vs. arbitrary change

local efficacy
vs. justified efficacy

preservation
vs. mere inertia

current success
vs. retained future corrigibility

capability growth
vs. capability growth that destroys correction channels
```

These distinctions are theoretical commitments or candidate constructs until independently measured.

## Authority boundary

The project has previously failed to establish an empirically irreducible correction-specific primitive within an audited phenomenon space. MAGIKARP therefore cannot be introduced as though a distinct correction-quality ontology has already been demonstrated.

Keep explicit:

```text
useful theory vocabulary
≠ validated construct

candidate transition property
≠ metric

conceptual coherence
≠ empirical independence
```

Any proposed MAGIKARP quantity must pass the same measurement discipline as every other object in this program.

## Relationship to the program

```text
CARS
→ how correction and research revision are performed

MAGIKARP
→ provisional theory of what could make transformation justified

ECIM-like descendant
→ first mechanism-level route for testing evidence-controlled, effective, isolated modification

Correction-Capable Adaptation
→ overarching scientific object
```

## Candidate theoretical target

A useful eventual notion may resemble **justified transformability**: the extent to which a system retains access to warranted future transformations after adapting.

But that phrase currently names a research direction, not an established observable.

Before promotion to a metric, the program would need at least:

```text
scientific proposition
measurement structure
admissible transformations
identified estimand
independent falsification
incremental validity beyond ordinary state/dynamics/control descriptions
```

Until then, MAGIKARP remains a theory layer that generates testable distinctions without inheriting empirical authority.
