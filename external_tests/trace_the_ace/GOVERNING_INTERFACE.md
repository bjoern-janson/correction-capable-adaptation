# Governing Trace the Ace interface

## Status

**FROZEN BRANCH-WIDE PROTOCOL**

This document governs all subsequent work under `external_tests/trace_the_ace/` unless a later, explicitly identified methodological revision is independently justified and prospectively authorized.

The external competition is an empirical consequence channel. It does not directly determine theoretical or ontological authority.

## Price is not authority

For candidate distinction `Z_k`, define the external consequence object:

\[
P_k = \text{external consequence under the frozen Trace the Ace evaluation regime}.
\]

Examples of components of `P_k` may include:

```text
Delta log loss
Delta calibration diagnostics
split robustness
source robustness
other prospectively specified consequence measures
```

But:

\[
\boxed{P_k \neq \operatorname{AUTH}(Z_k)}.
\]

**Price is consequence. Authority is diagnosed consequence.**

A leaderboard or held-out predictive result is therefore evidence input, not automatic modeling or theoretical authority.

## Only licensed propagation path

The only permitted evidence-propagation path is:

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal H_k
\rightarrow
\mathcal C_{k,prospective}
\rightarrow
\mathcal M_k
\rightarrow
P_k
\rightarrow
D_R
\rightarrow
D_H
\rightarrow
\operatorname{AUTH}(Z_k)
\rightarrow
\Delta W
}
\]

where:

- `H_k` is the localized hypothesis;
- `C_k,prospective` is the frozen prospective contract;
- `M_k` is the instantiated measurement/estimation apparatus;
- `P_k` is the external consequence;
- `D_R` diagnoses the result and its reliability/failure locus;
- `D_H` diagnoses what the result identifies about the hypothesis/construct;
- `AUTH(Z_k)` is the authority actually earned;
- `Delta W` is the licensed update to future hypothesis/model weights.

There is no shortcut:

\[
\boxed{P_k \not\rightarrow \Delta W_{CCA}}
\]

without the diagnostic layer.

## Directional safeguards

### Positive price

\[
\boxed{\text{positive price} \Rightarrow \text{attribution before credit}}
\]

A favorable predictive result cannot be interpreted as evidence for the intended construct until attribution, fidelity, identification, robustness, and nonredundancy requirements are satisfied.

A high-performing but poorly identified representation remains **predictively successful but theoretically unauthoritative**.

### Negative price

\[
\boxed{\text{negative price} \Rightarrow \text{localization before revision}}
\]

A null or adverse predictive result does not automatically reduce authority for the broader CCA construct. The failure locus must first be diagnosed among possibilities such as:

```text
hypothesis failure
operationalization failure
measurement/interface non-identifiability
estimation failure
redundancy
calibration failure
domain mismatch
execution/apparatus failure
```

Accordingly:

\[
G_{\Delta LL}=0 \not\Rightarrow \Delta W_{CCA}<0.
\]

## Authority is conjunctive and non-compensatory

For a candidate `Z_k`, durable modeling/theoretical authority is conjunctive rather than additive:

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{AUTH}(Z_k)
=
G_{\Delta LL}
\land
G_A
\land
G_R
\land
G_F
\land
G_I
\land
G_N
}
\]

with the gates interpreted prospectively for the experiment, including at minimum:

```text
G_DeltaLL  predictive consequence
G_A        attribution
G_R        robustness
G_F        fidelity to intended construct
G_I        identifiability
G_N        nonredundancy
```

These gates are non-compensatory. For example:

\[
G_F=0 \lor G_I=0
\Rightarrow
\operatorname{AUTH}(Z_k)=0
\]

regardless of how favorable the predictive price is.

## External-task authority boundary

\[
\boxed{\text{Trace the Ace has consequence authority, not ontological authority.}}
\]

Trace the Ace may establish statements such as:

> This prospectively operationalized distinction did or did not earn predictive support under this frozen evaluation regime.

It may not establish merely from price that:

```text
a CCA construct exists or does not exist
G1 is or is not real
PMC is or is not real
JT is or is not real
C_improve has or lacks validity
```

Likewise, an arbitrary high-performing feature may establish predictive structure in this task without acquiring authority to be relabeled as a CCA construct.

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{\text{external consequence can constrain theory without defining theory}.}
\]

## Governing compression

\[
\boxed{
\text{CCA generates bets;}\qquad
\text{Trace the Ace prices them;}\qquad
\text{CARS decides what the price is evidence for.}
}
\]

This is the governing interface between CCA, CARS, and the external prediction task.

## Scientific product: evidence topology

The scientific output of Trace the Ace is not merely a leaderboard ordering such as:

\[
M_1 < M_2 < M_3.
\]

It is an evidence topology:

\[
\boxed{\{\text{supported edges},\ \text{removed edges},\ \text{unresolved edges}\}.}
\]

Results may change this topology only along dimensions they identify.

A supported edge receives only the local authority earned by its gates. A removed edge is removed only for the prospectively tested operationalization/scope. An unresolved edge remains open rather than being collapsed into support or refutation.

## Consequence for future CCA-derived features

No `Z_E`, `Z_D`, `Z_C`, or `Z_P` may inherit authority from:

```text
objective main effects
semantic-capacity gains
leaderboard improvements
combined-model performance
other neighboring successes
```

Each candidate must be opened prospectively and tested against the independently earned mature non-CCA comparator, with attribution, robustness, fidelity, identifiability, and nonredundancy gates specified before execution.

A combined CCA model may not retroactively grant authority to components that failed or were never individually identified.

## Fixed methodological boundary

For all subsequent Trace the Ace work:

```text
price != authority
positive result -> attribution before credit
negative result -> localization before revision
external consequence -> diagnosis -> earned authority -> weight update
no direct leaderboard-to-theory shortcut
```

Any departure from this protocol is itself a methodological revision and must be made explicit rather than introduced implicitly through modeling convenience.
