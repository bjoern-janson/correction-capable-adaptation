# Research Returnability

## Status

**CANONICAL METHODOLOGICAL RULE — GOVERNS REOPENING, NOT RETROACTIVE RESULT REWRITING**

Correction-Capable Adaptation must remain capable of incorporating justified correction at the level of the research program itself.

The repository therefore treats every current conceptual, methodological, and scientific authority state as **reopenable under new discriminating evidence**.

\[
\boxed{\text{current authority}\neq\text{permanent immunity from revision}}
\]

This is research-program returnability.

## Core rule

> **Open the smallest boundary implicated by new discriminating evidence; revise what the evidence reaches; preserve everything not implicated; and preserve a warranted path for later reopening.**

A canonical, provisionally fixed, measurement-valid, or contract-frozen state records the program's current authority. It is not an assertion that future evidence is forbidden from challenging that state.

## Localization has priority

Reopening begins with CARS localization.

A deeper abstraction has no revision authority merely because it subsumes a shallower explanation.

\[
\boxed{\text{redescription}\neq\text{re-localization}}
\]

The shallowest sufficient failure locus retains priority. Deeper reopening requires either:

- new discriminating evidence that contradicts the localized account; or
- a discriminating residual that survives the localized revision and retest.

If no discriminating residual remains, no deeper opening is currently warranted.

\[
\boxed{r=0\Rightarrow\text{stop escalation}}
\]

## What `frozen` means

`CONTRACT_FROZEN` is an identity-preservation rule for an empirical object. It freezes the prospective choices under which that object is executed and interpreted.

It does **not** make the surrounding theory permanently unrevisable.

If new evidence warrants a different estimand, intervention, measurement structure, threshold, or scientific object, the program must create an explicit successor/amended object with preserved provenance rather than silently mutate the already executed contract.

Thus:

\[
\boxed{\text{frozen contract}\neq\text{frozen research program}}
\]

## What `closed` means

A `CLOSED` empirical result is immutable **as a historical result under its original identity and contract**.

Its recorded observations, estimands, analysis rules, and outcome must not be rewritten to rescue or reverse it.

But closure does not remove the subject from science. New evidence may justify:

- a descendant scientific object;
- a new contract;
- a narrower or broader scope claim;
- a revised mechanism hypothesis;
- a revised conceptual or methodological rule;
- a new experiment that explicitly supersedes an earlier interpretation.

The original result remains in the lineage.

\[
\boxed{\text{historical immutability}\neq\text{epistemic irreversibility}}
\]

## Canonical rules are reopenable

`CANONICAL` means current program authority, not permanent truth.

A canonical conceptual distinction or methodological rule may be amended or superseded when new discriminating evidence reaches that object. The revision must:

1. identify the failure or residual;
2. localize it at the shallowest sufficient level;
3. preserve the prior decision and evidence as provenance;
4. state exactly what authority is withdrawn, retained, or transferred;
5. create an explicit successor decision or amended rule;
6. retest any consequences that depend on the revised relation.

Mere elegance, broader explanatory scope, or a deeper unifying description is insufficient to reopen a stable local result.

## Returnability is not automatic execution

Reopening an object does not authorize implementation or execution.

Empirical execution still requires the lifecycle conditions in `RESEARCH_STATE_MACHINE.md`, including measurement validity, a frozen prospective contract, explicit authorization, and `implementation_authorized = true`.

## Program-level interpretation

The repo should remain open to correction while closed enough to preserve scientific identity, comparison, and provenance.

Operationally:

```text
new evidence
→ localize
→ discriminate
→ reopen the smallest implicated boundary
→ revise minimally in structural scope
→ preserve unaffected structure and historical provenance
→ retest
→ stop when no discriminating residual remains
```

The protected property is not a particular state. It is the research program's **returnability to justified inquiry**: its ability to bring any current claim back under disciplined adversarial review when future evidence actually earns that reopening.

This rule creates no new scientific primitive, metric, gate, empirical result, or execution authorization.
