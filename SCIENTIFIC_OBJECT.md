# Scientific Object

## Central research question

> **Can an adaptive system increase its future viability while remaining capable of incorporating justified correction?**

## Central object

**Correction-capable adaptation** is the capacity of an adaptive system to undergo warranted, effective change while preserving the conditions required for justified future correction.

The program studies warranted causal pathways from evidence to consequential change and onward to future correctability and transformability. One current decomposition is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Evidence}
\rightarrow
\text{Authority}
\rightarrow
\text{Warranted adaptive decision}
\rightarrow
\text{Effective modification}
\rightarrow
\text{Protected structure}
\rightarrow
\text{Post-Modification Correctability}
\rightarrow
\text{Repeated correction}
\rightarrow
\text{Justified Transformability}
\rightarrow
\text{Adaptive viability}
}
\]

Capability change may occur within this pathway, but capability alone does not define success.

The corresponding epistemic dependency is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{measurement}
\prec
\text{warranted causal control}
\prec
\text{validated causal composition}
\prec
\text{modification consequences}
\prec
\text{post-modification correctability}
\prec
\text{repeated correction evidence}
\prec
\text{transformability evidence}
\prec
\text{viability / capability}
\prec
\text{extreme-system stress tests}
}
\]

This is an evidence-ordering hierarchy. It must not automatically be read as a universal metaphysical decomposition of every adaptive architecture.

## CCA Causal Composition Principle

CCA canonically adopts:

> **A downstream causal claim may not inherit authority from an upstream validated relation across a separable transformation unless that transformation is independently identified, prospectively specified and validated as an apparatus guarantee, or explicitly excluded from the claim.**

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{\text{validated endpoints}\not\Rightarrow\text{validated pathway}.}
\]

This is a law of causal composition, not a new maturity level or universal intermediate mechanism.

Decision record: [`lineage/decisions/CCA_CAUSAL_COMPOSITION_PRINCIPLE.md`](lineage/decisions/CCA_CAUSAL_COMPOSITION_PRINCIPLE.md).

## Level 0 — Scientific-object and measurement validity

CCA provisionally adopts:

\[
\boxed{
G_1
=
\text{warranted evidence acquiring causal control over a separable adaptive decision}
}
\]

The current operational decomposition represents the separable adaptive decision as candidate selection:

\[
E\rightarrow C_{\mathrm{selected}}.
\]

This is the first empirical pathway CCA has chosen to study. It is not a claim that every correction-capable architecture must implement explicit candidate selection.

Decision record: [`lineage/decisions/G1_LEVEL0_ROLE.md`](lineage/decisions/G1_LEVEL0_ROLE.md).

The destructive Level-0 work established that codebook construction, relational sensitivity, representation invariance, access robustness, candidate accuracy, and mechanism/channel attribution are not substitutes for G1.

No concrete ontology, evidence set, realization policy, estimand, threshold, model, prompt, or benchmark is frozen by adopting the role.

## Level 1 — Evidence-controlled adaptive decision

Given prospectively admissible evidence and an independently warranted mapping, does causal reassignment of evidence move a separable adaptive decision in the warranted direction?

For the current operational instantiation:

\[
E=e\mapsto C^*(e),
\]

with observed choice \(C_{\mathrm{selected}}\).

Selection variability and absolute accuracy alone are insufficient. No Level-1 empirical result currently exists.

## Level 2 — Isolated modification

Modification effects must be identified independently of selection through direct assignment:

\[
\boxed{do(M=m)\rightarrow(Y_T,Y_P)}.
\]

Target efficacy and protected-behavior interference are distinct. Preservation remains an equivalence/noninterference claim with a prospectively frozen tolerance.

## Level 3 — Evidence → justified modification

Level 3 is an end-to-end causal-composition claim. It cannot be established merely because upstream G1 and downstream G2 assays are separately positive.

For a claimed path \(\pi\):

\[
\boxed{
\mathrm{ADVANCE}_{\pi}
\iff
G_1
\land
\mathrm{PathValid}(\pi)
\land
G_{2T}
\land
G_{2P}
}
\]

`PathValid(π)` requires every additional separable transformation crossed by the claim to be independently identified or prospectively specified and validated/verified as an apparatus relation.

## Level 4A — Post-Modification Correctability

CCA provisionally adopts **Post-Modification Correctability (PMC)** with the role:

> **After a consequential change, do the conditions required for future warranted correction remain available?**

Its causal content remains:

\[
\boxed{
\text{future admissible evidence}
\leadsto
\text{warranted causal authority}
\leadsto
\text{consequential correction}
}
\]

within a prospectively declared scope.

PMC is not current performance, capability, generic adaptability, plasticity, or one-shot correction success. It is not yet a scalar or metric.

Decision record: [`lineage/decisions/POST_MODIFICATION_CORRECTABILITY_ROLE.md`](lineage/decisions/POST_MODIFICATION_CORRECTABILITY_ROLE.md).

## Level 4B — Repeated correction

CCA canonically distinguishes:

\[
\boxed{\mathrm{PMC}\neq\mathrm{Repeated\ Correction}}
\]

or:

\[
\boxed{\text{capacity survived}\neq\text{capacity exercised}.}
\]

PMC is latent/dispositional availability. Repeated correction is a realized subsequent valid correction episode.

A valid repeated correction for opportunity \(e\) supports only local correction availability for \(e\), not broad PMC over an untested correction scope.

\[
\boxed{N_{\mathrm{corrections}}\neq C_{\mathrm{corr}}.}
\]

Decision record: [`lineage/decisions/PMC_REPEATED_CORRECTION_DISTINCTION.md`](lineage/decisions/PMC_REPEATED_CORRECTION_DISTINCTION.md).

No repeated-correction experiment is operationalized or authorized.

## Level 5 — Justified Transformability

CCA now provisionally adopts **Justified Transformability (JT)** as a distinct repertoire-level conceptual object.

Its current role is:

> **What prospectively relevant warranted transformations remain reachable from a system state, and can materially different warranted destinations be reached while preserving or reconstituting the causal conditions required for future warranted correction?**

The current conceptual separation is:

\[
\boxed{\mathrm{PMC}=\text{availability of future warranted correction}}
\]

\[
\boxed{\mathrm{RepeatedCorrection}=\text{realized temporal exercise}}
\]

\[
\boxed{\mathrm{JT}=\text{warranted transformation repertoire}}
\]

Repeated correction is not sufficient for JT:

\[
\boxed{\mathrm{RepeatedCorrection}\not\Rightarrow\mathrm{JT}.}
\]

A system may execute many locally valid or scripted corrections while remaining confined to one narrow dimension, fixed menu, or path-dependent basin, or while cumulatively destroying future transformation options.

The reverse is also not definitional. A prospectively assigned branching design could in principle establish materially different warranted transformations across replicated instances without one physical copy traversing a repeated temporal correction history:

\[
\boxed{\mathrm{JT}\not\Rightarrow\mathrm{ObservedRepeatedCorrectionHistory}.}
\]

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{\text{trajectory evidence}\neq\text{repertoire evidence}}
\]

and:

\[
\boxed{\text{logical object dependency}\neq\text{program evidence-ordering dependency}.}
\]

CCA may still require repeated correction before granting JT authority as a conservative research-order policy because repeated correction tests survival through actual consequential temporal change. That policy is not a universal definition of JT.

Decision record: [`lineage/decisions/JUSTIFIED_TRANSFORMABILITY_ROLE.md`](lineage/decisions/JUSTIFIED_TRANSFORMABILITY_ROLE.md).

### Unresolved JT semantics

This role does **not** freeze:

```text
what makes a transformation warranted
what makes destinations materially / appropriately different
target or transformation family
reachable-state / reachable-transformation formalism
novelty or distance thresholds
repertoire breadth / diversity / coverage
preservation versus reconstitution semantics
system / apparatus boundary
counterfactual availability criterion
estimand / estimator / metric / threshold
empirical protocol
```

JT must not degenerate into retrospective approval of whichever transformation occurred. Nor does state-space size establish warranted repertoire:

\[
\boxed{\text{many reachable states}\not\Rightarrow\text{many warranted transformations}.}
\]

`C_improve` remains downstream. JT does not establish that the repertoire improved because of valid feedback.

## Level 6 — Adaptive viability / capability

Do correction-capable trajectories preserve or expand prospectively defined viable futures?

No viability estimand is currently frozen. The provisional [`C_improve`](theory/C_IMPROVE.md) object remains a theory-level bridge, not a measured quantity.

## Level 7 — Extreme adaptive systems

AGI, recursive improvement, and ASI are possible stress-test regimes of the same scientific object. They are not foundational assumptions or currently authorized empirical targets.

## Measurement can constitute the object

The project treats measurement as part of scientific-object specification, not merely an estimator applied after the object is fully defined. Before testing invariance or equivalence, the admissible transformation class must be specified.
